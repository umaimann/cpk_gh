二次創作ガイドライン

【はじめに】

本ガイドラインは主に個人の方を対象としています。二次創作をご検討中の事業者の方は、https://twinengine.jp/contact/use/までお問い合わせください。

 

【『超かぐや姫！』の二次創作に対する基本スタンス】

★二次創作は是非楽しんでください。
★個人あるいは特定少数のグループで楽しむことを主たる目的としてください。
★作品ならびにファンを傷つける行為はご遠慮ください。
★ファンのみなさま同士で二次創作をもとに批判することはおやめください。
★本編設定と異なる場合は、誤解を生まないよう事前に注意書きをするなど留意してください。
★公序良俗に反する内容はお控えください。
★二次創作は創造性・オリジナル性が認められるものに限ります。また、公式の発表物・販売物と誤認されないようにしてください。

作中に「ツクヨミは皆が表現者」という言葉もあるように、『超かぐや姫！』としては今後も二次創作を楽しんでいただきたいと考えております。
創作される方、創作物を楽しまれる方、皆様が各方面にご配慮いただき、本ガイドラインを守ってお楽しみください。

 

【二次創作／二次創作物の定義】

二次創作・二次創作物とは、お客様がファン活動の一環として『超かぐや姫！』から得たイメージをもとに、お客様自身の思想または感情を創作的に表現する行為、または創作物のことをいいます。二次創作物または二次創作活動の例として、

★ファンアート（イラスト、漫画等）
★小説、考察、解釈創作
★コスプレ
★VRChat等でのワールド制作
★3Dモデル制作、アバター制作、改変
★音楽、ダンス、演出、ステージ制作
★配信、実況、切り抜き、紹介動画

などがあり、公式が権利を有する全ての著作物（一次創作物）や、公式から正式な商品化許諾を受けた商品・サービス（公式商品・公式ライセンス商品）とは区分されます。

Introduction

These guidelines are intended primarily for individual users. If you are a business considering the creation of derivative works, please contact us at this page: https://twinengine.jp/contact/use/.

Basic Stance on Fan Works for Cosmic Princess Kaguya!

Please feel free to enjoy creating fan works.
Fan works should be created primarily for enjoyment by individuals or small, specific groups.
Please refrain from actions that may harm the work itself or its fans.
Please do not criticize fellow fans based on their fan works.
If your work differs from the official story setting, please include a disclaimer or similar notice to avoid misunderstandings.
Please refrain from content that violates public order and morals.
Fan works should demonstrate creativity and originality in their expression. In addition, please ensure they are not mistaken for official publications or merchandise.

As reflected in the phrase “Everyone in Tsukuyomi is a creator” within the film, Cosmic Princess Kaguya! hopes fans will continue to enjoy creating fan works in the future as well.
Whether you create fan works or simply enjoy them, we ask that everyone be considerate toward others and have fun while following these guidelines.

Definition of Fan Works / Derivative Works

“Fan works” or “derivative works” refer to creative expressions made by users as part of fan activities inspired by Cosmic Princess Kaguya! in which they produce their own interpretations, ideas, or expressions. Examples of fan works or fan activities include:

Fan art (illustrations, comics, etc.)
Novels, analytical or interpretive works
Cosplay (costume play)
Creating worlds in VRChat and similar platforms
Creating or modifying 3D models and avatars
Music, dance, performances, and stage production
Streaming, commentary, clips, and introduction videos

Fan works and fan activities are distinct from all works officially owned by the rights holders (original works), as well as products and services officially authorized for commercialization (official products and licensed merchandise).

Sales and Distribution of Fan Works

Please see the following rules regarding the sales and distribution of fan works.

1, Doujinshi / Self-Published Fan Books
At doujinshi conventions held on or before December 31, 2026, fan activities are permitted only within the following scope:
Direct sales or distribution at convention venues
No digital sales, commission-based sales, or auction-based sales

* After on-site sales or distribution at conventions have ended, remaining stock may be sold via online sales. However, producing additional stock specifically for online sales is prohibited.
* Rules regarding sales or distribution at doujinshi conventions held on or after January 1, 2027 have not yet been finalized. Please wait for further official announcements.

2, Fan Goods
Regardless of whether for commercial or non-commercial purposes, sales or distribution of fan goods is not permitted. This is due to the difficulty of clearly distinguishing such goods from official products and officially licensed merchandise.

3, Digital Goods
Distribution is permitted only on a non-commercial basis. Commercial sales or distribution are prohibited.

4, Games
The creation, release, and distribution of games are prohibited regardless of whether they are commercial or non-commercial, or whether they are digital or analog, and regardless of their level of originality. However, use on Roblox is permitted only when the “Content Standards for the use of ‘Cosmic Princess Kaguya!’ on Roblox” are fully complied with, including creation, publication, and operation.
https://create.roblox.com/explore/licenses/72fb6d41-a299-4870-8d85-2807e084986d

5, Cosplay
Fans are welcome to enjoy costume creation, photography and filming, exhibitions, photo sharing, competition participation, and the publication of videos and related content as part of fan activities.
* Please refrain from purchasing cosplay costumes from unauthorized vendors.

6, Food Service, Cafés, and Other Events Involving Public Gathering
The following activities are prohibited:
★Providing food or beverages, or offering dining spaces to the public (whether to specified or unspecified individuals, or to small or large groups), is prohibited, regardless of commercial or non-commercial purpose and whether the items are homemade or commercially prepared.
★Organizing events for commercial purposes that gather the public (whether to specified or unspecified individuals, or to small or large groups) at specific locations.

Use of Original Works

Regarding images and videos published on the official Cosmic Princess Kaguya! accounts on X (formerly Twitter), YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, users may repost or share all or part of such content on websites, blogs, or social media platforms (including embedding on external platforms) for non-commercial purposes.

For derivative works using music from Cosmic Princess Kaguya! (including lyrics and compositions, i.e., musical copyrights) as well as sound recordings (master recordings included on CDs or digital distribution), such as arrangements, MAD videos, or performances, please follow the rules below.
★For videos such as “dance covers” or “song covers” using the music, please use services that handle rights clearance through NexTone or JASRAC. For more details, please refer to the official pages:
https://www.nex-tone.co.jp/copyright/users/int/youtube_precautions.html
https://www.jasrac.or.jp/info/network/pickup/movie.html
★For “dance covers” or “song covers” using sound recordings, usage is permitted only for materials distributed on Piapro, and only under the conditions specified there:
https://piapro.jp/Cho_KaguyaHime

When using original works, please note the following:
1, Excessive modification of original materials is not permitted.
2, Commercial use of original works is prohibited. However, advertising revenue from platforms such as YouTube is not considered commercial use.
3, Copying, scanning, sampling, or tracing official merchandise or officially licensed product materials is strictly prohibited.
4, Uploading any part or all of Netflix-distributed footage, or extracting still images via screenshots, is prohibited.
5, Please ensure that posted content does not distort or misrepresent the image of Cosmic Princess Kaguya!, and does not harm its reputation or brand image.

Prohibited Activities

To protect the integrity and worldbuilding of Cosmic Princess Kaguya!, its production team, and fan activities, the following actions are strictly prohibited without exception:
★Any conduct that misrepresents Cosmic Princess Kaguya! or harms its image.

★Any activity that falsely claims to be or could be mistaken as official or affiliated with Cosmic Princess Kaguya!

Examples:
●Creating accounts or posts on X (formerly Twitter) that appear to be made by the characters from Cosmic Princess Kaguya!
●Running initiatives that could be mistaken as official Cosmic Princess Kaguya! projects.

★Any conduct that falsely implies or suggests authorization, sponsorship, or partnership with Cosmic Princess Kaguya! staff or affiliates.

★Posting or sharing content that misrepresents, distorts, or misinterprets these guidelines.

★Any use of the series logo in fan works that could be confused with official or licensed products.

★Copying, scanning, sampling, or tracing materials from official merchandise or licensed products.

★Commercial use of original works in derivative fan creations.

★The sales or distribution of derivative works such as fan goods or games.

★Organizing food service events, cafés, or similar commercial events aimed at attracting customers.

★Uploading any part or all of Netflix-distributed footage, or sharing screenshots or extracts.

★Any conduct that damages the reputation, dignity, or rights of third parties.

★Any violation of public order or social etiquette (including trespassing on private property, littering, noise disturbance, or unauthorized filming/streaming that infringes privacy, etc.).

★Creating derivative works that include specific political, ideological, or religious content.

★Creating derivative works that include content against public order and morals or antisocial themes.

★Any other conduct deemed inappropriate by the official team.

Final Notes

These guidelines do not apply to works created, sold, or published prior to their announcement. However, if the official team reasonably determines that such works fall under prohibited activities, you may be asked to modify, remove, suspend, or recall them.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation going forward.

These guidelines are subject to change or updates without prior notice. Please refer to the latest version from time to time and enjoy creating fan works in accordance with the most up-to-date guidelines.

We ask that you refrain from engaging in activities that do not comply with these guidelines, and that you avoid directly confronting or correcting others based on their contents. However, as it is not possible to address every individual case, users may at times exchange opinions. In such cases, we ask that communication be conducted with consideration and respect for others.
We ask that each and every one of you follow these guidelines when creating fan works.
Please use your own judgment for matters not explicitly covered by these guidelines. If you are unsure, we ask that you refrain from using the content.

The official team assumes no responsibility for any damages arising from the publication, modification, or updates of these guidelines.
Notwithstanding the provisions above, the official team may, if deemed necessary, request that individual users cease creating or using fan works on a case-by-case basis. The official team shall not be held responsible for any consequences arising from such requests.
Please note that we are unable to respond to individual inquiries regarding these guidelines, including opinions and reports.

These guidelines are published in Japanese. Translations into other languages are provided for reference only. In the event of any discrepancy between the Japanese version and any translation, the Japanese version shall prevail.

Thank you for your continued support of Cosmic Princess Kaguya!
